# Elevator Pitch

Laundry Capacity Network (LCN) is a platform that connects independent laundry businesses, neighborhood Drop Points, and customers into one collaborative ecosystem.

Unlike existing laundry marketplaces, LCN also enables laundries to share excess workloads with other verified laundries through a Capacity Exchange system.

Instead of competing with laundries, LCN aims to become the operating system that helps independent laundries grow together.

---

# Vision

Become Indonesia's largest collaborative network for independent laundries.

Rather than owning washing machines or opening franchise stores, LCN builds the digital infrastructure connecting existing businesses.

---

# Problem Statement

Indonesia has thousands of independent laundry businesses.

Most experience one of two problems.

## Overloaded Laundry

- Too many orders
- Late delivery
- Customer complaints
- Lost revenue

## Underutilized Laundry

- Machines idle
- Employees waiting
- Low machine utilization
- Poor profitability

Today these laundries operate independently.

There is no standard platform that allows them to collaborate.

---

# Proposed Solution

LCN introduces a Capacity Exchange.

Example

Laundry A

Capacity: 100 kg/day

Orders: 150 kg/day

↓

Overflow 50 kg

↓

Platform routes jobs

↓

Laundry B

Capacity: 100 kg/day

Orders: 40 kg/day

↓

Remaining Capacity: 60 kg

Result

- Laundry A keeps customers.
- Laundry B earns more.
- Customer receives clothes on time.
- Platform earns revenue.

# Ecosystem

There are three primary participants.

## Laundry Partner

Independent laundry business.

Responsibilities

- Wash clothes
- Accept Capacity Exchange jobs
- Maintain quality standards

Benefits

- More customers
- Better machine utilization
- Additional revenue
- Business management tools

---

## Drop Point

Neighborhood businesses acting as pickup and drop-off locations.

Examples

- Grocery stores
- Boarding houses
- Apartment lobbies
- Existing laundries

Benefits

- Additional income
- More customer traffic

---

## Customer

Uses the application to place laundry orders.

Benefits

- Easier ordering
- More locations
- Better tracking
- Standardized service

# Business Philosophy

The company optimizes for:

1. Positive cash flow.
2. Partner growth.
3. Sustainable recurring revenue.
4. Network effects.

The company does NOT optimize for:

- Owning laundry stores.
- Owning washing machines.
- Building logistics infrastructure.
- Fast nationwide expansion.

# Current Assumptions

Current assumptions include:

- Laundry owners are willing to pay registration.
- Laundry owners are willing to pay monthly membership.
- Capacity Exchange creates higher partner retention.
- Customers care more about reliability than brand.
- Partner density is more important than nationwide coverage.
- Positive cash flow is more important than rapid scaling during the first year.

These assumptions have not yet been validated.
