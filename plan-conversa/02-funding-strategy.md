## Two Approaches

### Approach A: Customer First, Investor Later

**What:** Pitch Tobias as a paying customer. Use that relationship to prove value, then ask for investment in 3-4 months.

**Pitch to Tobias (Phase 1):**
> *"I built an AI chatbot platform. You need one for Proctor. I'll integrate it for €1,800, then you subscribe at standard price ($69-99/mo). First month free, no lock-in."*

**Pitch to Tobias (Phase 2, Month 3-4):**
> *"You've seen it work. Now I'm raising a small pre-seed to build WhatsApp and scale. €500K cap SAFE, €10-15K minimum. I'm waiving your subscription for 18 months as part of the deal."*

**Timeline:**

```
Month 1:  Customer (€1,800 + $69/mo)
Month 2:  Using it daily — support tickets dropping
Month 3:  Show Tobias his own analytics: conversations handled, deflection rate
Month 4:  Pitch investment → €10-15K SAFE closes
         ↓
Total from Tobias: ~€13-18K over 4 months
```

**Pros:**
- Lowest risk for Tobias — easy "yes" upfront
- He gets to see real value before committing more
- You build a case study at standard pricing (validates your model)
- Clean separation of conversations

**Cons:**
- Money comes in slowly (€1,800 now, rest months later)
- No guarantee he invests after 3 months
- Time spent proving yourself

---

### Approach B: Bundled Pitch (Customer + Investment Together)

**What:** Pitch both in one conversation. He pays for integration AND invests at the same time.

**Pitch to Tobias:**
> *"We built an AI chatbot platform. You need one for Proctor. Here's what I'm proposing:*
>
> *1. I integrate Conversa into Proctor — €1,800*
> *2. You invest €10K via SAFE (€500K cap) — we waive your subscription for 24 months*
> *3. Total: €11,800 — you get a working chatbot + equity in the company*
>
> *Building this chatbot from scratch: €15-20K. This way you get the product AND a stake."*

**Timeline:**

```
Day 1:  One conversation → €11,800 total
Day 7:  Integration starts
Day 14: Proctor goes live with Conversa
```

**Pros:**
- Money comes in immediately (€11,800)
- No waiting for proof
- Simpler — one pitch, one decision

**Cons:**
- Harder ask upfront — requires more trust
- Tobias might hesitate without seeing it work first
- Risk of "no" to everything vs "yes" to just the monthly plan

| Likelihood of "yes" | High (Phase 1) + Medium (Phase 2) | Medium |

| Relationship | Slow build, natural progression | "Salesy" — could feel pushy |

## Recommendation

**Go with Approach A unless you're certain Tobias is ready to write a check today.**

Reasons:
1. Low-risk first ask = higher chance of "yes"
2. He's a fellow founder — he appreciates the "prove it first" approach
3. You avoid the awkwardness of asking for €10K+ without proof
4. You get a case study at standard pricing (proves your model)

But **prepare Phase 2 (investment ask) now.** Share it with Yohakim so you're aligned:

## Legal Structure — You + Yohakim

You have two existing entities:
- **CV Estro Hutama** (you)
- **PT** (Yohakim)

This is your first project together. Options:

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Create new PT** for Conversa | Clean, equal, purpose-built | Costs ~€500-1K, takes weeks |
| **Use one existing entity** + profit share agreement | Fast, zero cost | Uneven ownership, trust-based |
| **Joint venture agreement** between CV + PT | Equal, no new entity | Tax complexity, bank account issues |

**Suggestion:** Start with a simple **operating agreement** between CV Estro Hutama + Yohakim's PT. Split equity 50/50 (or whatever you agree). Use one entity's bank account temporarily. If you raise >€25K later, create a new PT.
