# proctOR Knowledge Base 04 — Company Module & Enterprise Clients

> Audience: Hospital executives, surgical centers, device OEMs, company admins, and chatbot training.
> Purpose: How institutions run a private, sponsor-funded surgical training network on proctOR.

---

## 1. What the Company Module Is

- The **Company Module** is a private, invitation-only marketplace layer for healthcare organizations:
  - Hospitals and surgical centers
  - Medical device manufacturers (OEMs)
  - Teaching/residency programs
  - Specialty societies and training institutes
- Companies run their own surgical training network in an **isolated environment**, completely invisible to the open Public Marketplace.

---

## 2. Onboarding a Company

### 2.1 Companies Cannot Self-Register

- Company accounts are **created and configured manually by proctOR Super Admins** after a sales/intake conversation.
- Why: to verify the institution, define scope, agree contract terms, and provision the isolated workspace correctly.

### 2.2 Provisioning Steps

1. Commercial agreement signed (subscription + coin packages).
2. proctOR Super Admin creates the Company Workspace.
3. Company admin accounts are created and granted dashboard roles.
4. Verified proctOR doctors are invited into the private pool.

---

## 3. The Company Workspace & Dashboard

### 3.1 Key Capabilities

- **Invite verified doctors** into the private company pool (mentors must already be verified on proctOR — KB-01).
- **Create and schedule sessions** on behalf of mentors.
- **Manage members**: add/remove residents, mentees, and mentors.
- **Monitor**:
  - Member participation
  - Active and completed session statistics
  - Coin balances across the company pool
  - Spending and training utilization reports
- **Sponsor training**: transfer coins from the Company Wallet directly to employee/resident Mentee wallets.

### 3.2 Visibility Isolation

- Everything inside a Company Marketplace — sessions, mentors, member activity, coin flows — is **invisible to the Public Marketplace** and to other companies.
- Each company's data is strictly isolated.

### 3.3 Dual Membership

- An individual expert surgeon can belong to the **Public Marketplace** *and* **multiple private Company Marketplaces** simultaneously, when granted independent access by each organization.

---

## 4. Sponsorship & Coin Flows

### 4.1 The Company Wallet

- Each company has a **Company Wallet** holding bulk-purchased coins.
- Companies buy **bulk coin packages** at the same €10/coin ratio, with volume discounts for large packages.

### 4.2 Atomic Coin Transfers

- Companies transfer coins **atomically** from the Company Wallet to specific employee/resident wallets.
- Transfers are instantaneous, ledger-recorded, and auditable — ideal for granting training budgets at scale.
- Sponsorship can be:
  - **Per-resident**: fixed budget per mentee
  - **Per-program**: budget tied to a training program or course
  - **Ad hoc**: one-off grants for specific sessions

### 4.3 What Sponsored Coins Can Be Used For

- Booking preceptorship/proctorship sessions within the company network **or** with vetted public mentors (per company policy).
- Company policy can restrict whether sponsored coins can be used outside the private pool.

## 5. Pricing Model (B2E)

| Item | Model |
|---|---|
| **Company Module access** | Flat **monthly subscription fee** per private marketplace instance |
| **Training coins** | Bulk coin packages at €10/coin, volume discounts available |
| **Sponsorship** | Included via Company Wallet → employee wallet transfers |
| **Contracts** | Annual terms typical; invoiced, EU-compliant |

- B2E is a **high-margin SaaS** model: recurring subscription + high-volume coin consumption.

## 6. Use Cases

### 6.1 Hospital Residency Program

A teaching hospital runs a private network for residents to get supervised remote guidance from staff and external experts — fully tracked, sponsored, and invisible to competitors.

### 6.2 Device OEM Training

A medical device manufacturer uses proctOR to let surgeons proctor other surgeons on a newly launched device — scaling certification coverage across Europe without flying trainers.

### 6.3 Specialty Society

A surgical society runs a private preceptorship program for members, sponsored by membership fees, with utilization reporting.

## 7. Common Questions (FAQ)

| Question | Response |
|---|---|
| Can our hospital create an account ourselves? | No — company accounts are provisioned by proctOR Super Admins after a commercial agreement. |
| Can other hospitals see our mentors and sessions? | No. Company marketplaces are completely isolated and invisible to the public marketplace and to other companies. |
| Can we invite a surgeon who isn't on proctOR? | They must first self-register and pass medical credential verification before joining your private pool. |
| How do we fund our residents' training? | Buy bulk coins into the Company Wallet, then transfer coins directly to resident wallets — atomically and auditable. |
| Can one mentor work with multiple hospitals? | Yes — dual membership allows a verified surgeon to belong to the public marketplace and multiple company marketplaces. |
| What does the Company Module cost? | A flat monthly subscription plus bulk coin packages. Volume discounts apply. |
| Do we get usage reports? | Yes — dashboards track participation, session stats, and coin utilization. |
