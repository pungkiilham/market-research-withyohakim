# proctOR Master Knowledge Base & Context Specification

This document provides a comprehensive, structured knowledge base for the **proctOR** platform[cite: 2]. It covers operational parameters, business logic, compliance rules, and workflows across all platform stakeholders: **Public Visitors**, **Medical Users (Mentors & Mentees)**, **Enterprise & Company Clients**, and **Potential Investors**[cite: 2].

---

## SECTION 1: Public Visitors & Guest Experience

### 1.1 Core Platform Overview
* **What is proctOR?**: proctOR is a mobile-first digital marketplace application designed to connect medical professionals for remote surgical support through structured preceptorship and proctorship models[cite: 2].
* **Primary Mission**: To professionalize, standardize, and scale surgical mentorship globally by providing a centralized scheduling, monetization, and communication infrastructure without hosting sensitive medical content or storing patient data[cite: 2].
* **Core Value Proposition**: Operates as a pure management and coordination platform—connecting expert surgeons with practicing physicians who need live remote guidance or peer consultation[cite: 2].

### 1.2 Data Privacy, Security & GDPR Compliance
* **Zero Patient Data Storage**: proctOR **does not store, process, host, or record** any medical data or patient-identifiable information anywhere on its servers[cite: 2].
* **GDPR Compliance**: Designed for European deployment with strict adherence to EU data residency and privacy mandates[cite: 2].
* **Live Video vs. Smart Glasses**:
  * **Surgical Streaming**: Live surgery procedure feeds are streamed via third-party smart glass applications using link-based access manually shared in-app[cite: 2]. proctOR does not process or store the stream[cite: 2].
  * **In-App Consultation**: Integrated third-party video calls (Zoom SDK) are strictly for pre/post-session consultations and quick communications[cite: 2]. Video calls are live-only, unrecorded, and store no video data[cite: 2].

## SECTION 2: Medical Users (Mentors & Mentees)

### 2.1 Role Mechanics & Account Verification
* **Dual-Role Flexibility**: A verified doctor can act as a **Mentee** in one specialized area and serve as a **Mentor** in another area where they hold high expertise[cite: 2].
* **Registration & Onboarding**:
  * Self-registration is available via iOS and Android apps by providing full name, email, role preferences, and medical specialty[cite: 2].
  * All accounts require mandatory credential verification (medical license upload/sign credentials) by proctOR Medical Reviewers before platform features unlock[cite: 2].

### 2.2 Preceptorship vs. Proctorship Workflow

| Feature / Model | Preceptorship | Proctorship |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initiator** | Created by **Mentor** as a session offer[cite: 2]. | Created by **Mentee** as a request for help[cite: 2]. |
| **Participant Capacity** | Mentor can define participant capacity limits[cite: 2]. | Mentee can invite multiple candidate mentors[cite: 2]. |
| **Booking & Acceptance** | Mentee books $\rightarrow$ Coins automatically reserved[cite: 2]. Mentors **cannot** reject bookings once published[cite: 2]. | Mentee negotiates details via chat and selects mentor(s)[cite: 2]. |
| **Payout Mechanics** | Reserved coins transfer to mentor upon confirmation of session completion[cite: 2]. | Each participating mentor is paid individually[cite: 2]. |

### 2.3 Coin, Wallet & Financial Rules
* **Internal Currency Unit**: $1\text{ Coin} = €10\text{ value}$[cite: 2].
* **Purchasing**: Mentees buy coins in-app via Shopify integration (invoicing handled by Shopify)[cite: 2].
* **Escrow Holding**: Coins are **reserved** in escrow from the mentee's wallet at booking[cite: 2]. They are transferred to the mentor's wallet **only after** the mentor confirms session completion[cite: 2].
* **Cancellation & Refund Rules**:
  * **$>24\text{ hours}$ before session**: Full coin refund to mentee's wallet[cite: 2].
  * **Mentor cancels**: Full coin refund to mentee's wallet regardless of timing[cite: 2].
  * **$<24\text{ hours}$ before session**: Canceled by mentee $\rightarrow$ No refund; reserved coins are forfeited[cite: 2].
  * **Refund Medium**: Refunds are credited back strictly as **Coins**, never converted back to cash directly[cite: 2].
* **Mentor Cash-Out**: Mentors submit manual payout requests from their wallet, which are audited and processed by proctOR Finance Admins within defined business days[cite: 2].
* **Ratings & Reputation**: Post-session ratings are mandatory for both parties[cite: 2]. Simple deterministic rankings (rating average, completion rate, session count) determine mentor marketplace visibility[cite: 2].

## SECTION 3: Company & Enterprise Clients (Hospitals & Device OEMs)

### 3.1 The Private Marketplace Module
* **Private Ecosystem**: Healthcare organizations (hospitals, surgical centers, device manufacturers) operate in an isolated **Company Marketplace** environment[cite: 2].
* **Visibility Isolation**: Sessions, mentors, and activities within a Company Marketplace are completely invisible to the open Public Marketplace[cite: 2].
* **Dual Membership for Mentors**: An individual expert surgeon can belong to the Public Marketplace **and** multiple private Company Marketplaces simultaneously if granted independent access[cite: 2].

### 3.2 Enterprise Administration & Sponsorship
* **Onboarding Process**: Companies **cannot self-register**[cite: 2]. Accounts are created and configured manually by proctOR Super Admins[cite: 2].
* **Company Dashboard Features**:
  * Invite existing verified proctOR doctors into their private company pool[cite: 2].
  * Create and schedule preceptorship/proctorship sessions directly on behalf of mentors[cite: 2].
  * Monitor member participation, active session statistics, and coin balances[cite: 2].
* **Sponsorship & Coin Transfers**:
  * Companies pay a flat monthly subscription fee for access to the module[cite: 2].
  * Companies buy bulk coin packages and can transfer coins directly from the Company Wallet to employee/resident Mentee wallets to sponsor their surgical training[cite: 2].

## SECTION 4: Potential Investors & Strategic Partners

### 4.1 Market Need & Solution
* **The Industry Gap**: Traditional surgery mentorship and proctoring rely on manual coordination, unmanaged payments, fragmented chat apps, lack of calendar visibility, and zero standardized reputation tracking[cite: 2].
* **The proctOR Solution**: A scalable, compliant, mobile-first marketplace that centralizes session scheduling, automated coin escrow, 2-way calendar sync, and secure in-app communication[cite: 2].

### 4.2 Business Model & Revenue Streams
* **B2C Marketplace**: 50% spread/commission between coin purchase price and mentor payout[cite: 2].
* **B2E Corporate SaaS**: Flat monthly subscription fee for private company marketplace instances plus bulk coin package sales for sponsored mentees[cite: 2].

### 4.3 Scalability & Defensibility
* **Zero Medical Liability Risk**: By intentionally omitting medical/patient data storage and utilizing external link-based smart glass streams, proctOR eliminates medical data liability while remaining fully GDPR compliant[cite: 2].
* **Demand-Driven Growth (Ticket System)**: When mentees search for an unavailable specialty, an automated ticket is logged in the Operator Dashboard[cite: 2]. This gives proctOR real-time market demand data to target and recruit in-demand medical specialists[cite: 2].
* **Expansion Roadmap**:
  * **Phase 1 (Current)**: European launch (Germany priority), core coin escrow engine, 2-way calendar sync, manual payouts, company modules[cite: 2].
  * **Phase 2 (Future)**: Automated payout integrations, AI-based mentor/specialty matching algorithms, and self-service company onboarding[cite: 2].
