# proctOR Knowledge Base 08 — Investor & Strategic Partner Pitch

> Audience: Potential investors, strategic partners, and chatbot training.
> Purpose: The investor-facing narrative: the problem, the market, the model, the moat, and the roadmap.

---

## 1. The Problem (Market Gap)

- Surgical mentorship and proctoring today run on **manual coordination**: emails, scattered chat apps, phone calls, no shared calendar, no standardized payments.
- **No reputation system** — surgeons can't verify who's reliable, and mentees can't compare mentors.
- **No structured market** — training across borders and institutions is fragmented and unprofessionalized.
- Hospitals and device OEMs have **no compliant, scalable way** to run remote preceptorship networks at volume.

### The Industry Pain Points

| Pain | Today's Reality |
|---|---|
| Scheduling | Fragmented calendars, double-booking, timezone friction |
| Payments | No escrow, no fair cancellation, no transparent fees |
| Verification | No standardized way to confirm a doctor is licensed |
| Reputation | No comparable, trusted track record |
| Compliance | No GDPR-safe way to stream without touching patient data |

---

## 2. The Solution

proctOR is a **scalable, compliant, mobile-first marketplace** that centralizes:

- Session **scheduling** with 2-way calendar sync
- **Coin escrow** with immutable, auditable ledger
- **Secure in-app communication** (unrecorded video + link-based smart-glass streaming)
- **Mandatory medical verification** of every user
- **Deterministic reputation & ranking**
- A **private Company Module** for institutions and OEMs

---

## 3. Business Model & Revenue Streams

| Stream | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| **B2C Public Marketplace** | ~**50% spread/commission** between coin purchase price (€10/coin) and mentor payout rate. Every session booked generates platform revenue. |
| **B2E Company SaaS** | **Flat monthly subscription** per private marketplace instance + **bulk coin package** sales for sponsored mentees. Recurring, high-margin. |
| **Future streams** | Premium tiers, AI matching, automated payouts, expanded verticals. |

### Unit Economics Highlights

- Marketplace spread is **per-transaction and immediately profitable** at volume.
- B2E SaaS is **recurring revenue** with high retention (training programs run all year).
- Low operational cost per session: no clinical data handling, no recording storage.

## 5. Scalability & Defensibility

### 5.1 Zero Medical Liability Risk

- By **intentionally omitting medical/patient data storage** and using external link-based smart-glass streams, proctOR eliminates medical data liability while remaining fully GDPR compliant.
- This is a **structural moat**: competitors who build on medical data inherit regulatory risk; proctOR does not.

### 5.2 Demand-Driven Growth

- The **ticket system** turns market signals into targeted mentor recruitment — a self-improving flywheel (KB-06 §5).

### 5.3 Network Effects

- More mentors → better coverage → more mentees → more revenue → attracts more mentors.
- Verified professional network + reputation data = compounding switching costs.

### 5.4 First-Mover Advantage

- proctOR is professionalizing a market that currently runs on ad-hoc tools. Being first to define standards (verification, escrow, reputation) in this niche creates lasting positioning.
