# proctOR Knowledge Base 07 — Support, Disputes & Human Escalation

> Audience: Mentors, mentees, company admins, support agents, and chatbot training.
> Purpose: How users get help, how disputes are resolved, and how the Conversa chatbot escalates to a human.

---

## 1. Getting Help on proctOR

| Need | Channel |
|---|---|
| **Instant answers** | The proctOR chatbot (Conversa) — answers platform questions from the knowledge base. |
| **Verified issue / account help** | In-app support ticket → proctOR Support Team. |
| **Verification questions** | Support ticket routed to Medical Reviewers. |
| **Payment / payout questions** | Support ticket routed to Finance Admins. |
| **Urgent or unresolved** | Direct escalation to a human agent (chat handoff). |

---

## 2. The Conversa Chatbot Experience

- The chatbot greets users in-app and on the web with a professional, consultative tone (KB persona doc).
- It answers from the proctOR knowledge base: onboarding, sessions, wallet, compliance, company module.
- It **never guesses**: when it lacks confidence, it says so and offers a human handoff.
- It **remembers returning users** in-context (e.g., "you mentioned you're a mentee in orthopedics") to give better answers — without storing medical or patient data.

### 2.1 When the Bot Escalates to a Human

- User explicitly asks for a human
- User reports a technical or financial issue (booking errors, missing coins, payout problems)
- User reports a dispute or a safety concern
- User's issue is outside the platform's supported domain

---

## 3. Support Ticket Lifecycle

1. User submits a support ticket (or is escalated from chat).
2. Ticket is categorized and routed (Support / Medical Review / Finance / Company).
3. Agent responds within target SLA (e.g., <24h business days; urgent issues same-day).
4. Resolution logged; user can rate the resolution.

---

## 4. Dispute Resolution

### 4.1 What Can Be Disputed

- Session quality / mentor performance
- Session not delivered or abandoned
- Coin handling: unexpected reservations, refunds, or forfeits
- Payout discrepancies
- Company wallet / sponsorship issues

### 4.2 Dispute Flow

1. User files a dispute via chat or support, with a clear description and any evidence (screenshots of the schedule/ledger).
2. **proctOR moderators** review the session history, ledger records, and both parties' statements.
3. Because the **ledger is immutable**, coin movement can be reconstructed exactly — disputes are decided on facts.
4. Outcome options:
   - **Refund coins** to the mentee wallet
   - **Release coins** to the mentor
   - **Partial split**, depending on findings
   - **Action on reputation** (e.g., rating/review moderation, temporary restrictions for abusive behavior)
5. Both parties are informed of the decision with reasoning.

### 4.3 Escalation Levels

- **Level 1**: Chatbot self-service + FAQ.
- **Level 2**: Support agent resolves.
- **Level 3**: Moderator team decides formal disputes.
- **Level 4**: Company client escalations handled with dedicated account managers.

---

## 5. Fairness & Transparency

- Refunds follow the published cancellation policy (KB-02 §5) — no exceptions without moderator review.
- Both parties can submit evidence; decisions are reasoned and recorded.
- Abusive reviews or behavior are actioned to protect marketplace integrity (KB-06).

---

## 6. Common Questions (FAQ)

| Question | Response |
|---|---|
| I lost coins on a booking. How do I get them back? | Check the cancellation policy first. For unexpected issues, file a dispute — moderators review the immutable ledger. |
| A mentor didn't show up. What now? | You're entitled to a full refund (mentor cancellation). Contact support and the refund is processed as coins. |
| Can I get my money back as cash? | No — refunds are always credited as coins to your wallet. |
| How long does a dispute take? | Typically 2–5 business days, depending on the evidence needed. |
| Can I escalate from chat to a human? | Yes — ask for a human, or the bot will escalate automatically for financial or technical issues. |
| Who decides disputes? | proctOR moderators, using the session history and the immutable coin ledger. |
