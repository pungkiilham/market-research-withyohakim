# proctOR Knowledge Base 01 — Onboarding & Medical Credential Verification

> Audience: Medical users (mentors & mentees), hospital administrators, and chatbot training.
> Purpose: Everything the Conversa chatbot must know about how a doctor joins proctOR, how roles work, and how verification protects the network.

---

## 1. Who Can Join proctOR

- proctOR is **medical-professionals only**. Access to the marketplace, sessions, and payments requires a **verified medical license**.
- Target users: practicing surgeons, senior clinicians, residents in training, and fellows seeking supervised practice in a new procedure or specialty.
- Platform language: English-first interface with multi-language support in-app; EU deployment with German priority.

---

## 2. Registration & Account Creation

### 2.1 Self-Registration Flow (iOS / Android)

1. Download the proctOR app from the App Store / Google Play (or access the web registration form).
2. Provide:
   - Full legal name
   - Professional email address (institutional email recommended)
   - Medical specialty / subspecialty
   - Preferred role preference(s) — **Mentor**, **Mentee**, or **Both (dual-role)**
3. Create a password and accept the Terms of Service + Privacy Policy.
4. Account is created in a **pending-verification** state — no marketplace features are unlocked yet.

### 2.2 What Happens Next

- The user receives a confirmation email to verify their email address.
- The user submits their **medical credentials** (see §3) through the in-app verification flow.
- Until verification completes, the doctor **cannot** publish sessions, book sessions, or transact coins.

---

## 3. Medical Credential Verification (Mandatory)

### 3.1 What Must Be Submitted

- A copy of the valid **medical license** (national license / approbation where applicable).
- Specialty certification / board certification documents (when available).
- Optional supporting evidence: employment letter from a recognized hospital, institutional verification letter, or reference from an already-verified proctOR mentor.

### 3.2 The Review Process

- Submitted credentials are reviewed **manually by proctOR Medical Reviewers** — not automated, by design.
- Reviewer checks:
  - Document authenticity and completeness
  - That the name and specialty match the registered profile
  - That the license is currently valid (not expired or suspended)
- Typical review completion: **1–3 business days** for straightforward submissions.
- Incomplete or unclear documents are returned with a clear reason; the doctor can re-submit.

### 3.3 After Verification

- Verified doctors gain full access:
  - Publish **preceptorship** offers (as Mentor)
  - Post **proctorship** requests (as Mentee)
  - Book sessions, hold coins in their wallet, and request payouts
  - Appear in marketplace search results and public profiles
- A verified doctor receives a **Verified** badge on their public profile.

### 3.4 Re-verification & Ongoing Validity

- Licenses are re-checked periodically (annual renewal reminders) or when proctOR receives credible notice of a change in a doctor's status.
- ProctOR reserves the right to suspend or revoke access if a credential expires, is revoked, or a user violates the Code of Conduct.

---

## 4. Roles & Dual-Role Flexibility

| Role | What They Do |
|---|---|
| **Mentor** | Publishes preceptorship session offers; provides live remote surgical guidance; sets participant capacity; receives coin payouts after confirmed completion. |
| **Mentee** | Publishes proctorship requests for help; books preceptorship sessions; holds and spends coins; negotiates details via chat. |
| **Dual-Role (Verified Doctor)** | Can act as **Mentor in one specialty** where they hold high expertise, and **Mentee in another** where they want supervised practice. Roles are per-specialty, not global. |

### 4.1 Dual-Role Rules

- A doctor can switch context per session — the platform does not force a single identity.
- Ratings and reputation are tracked **per role and per specialty** so a surgeon's mentoring reputation is not confused with their mentoring record.
- Wallet is a single wallet per account, but coins spent as Mentee and coins earned as Mentor are tracked separately in the transaction ledger.

## 5. Common Questions (FAQ)

| Question | Response |
|---|---|
| Can a medical student join proctOR? | No. Only licensed, verified medical professionals can access the marketplace. Students cannot register until they hold a valid license. |
| How long does verification take? | Usually 1–3 business days once all documents are complete. |
| Can I change my specialty later? | Yes, but new specialties are subject to re-verification of relevant credentials. |
| Is my license document safe? | Yes. Credentials are stored encrypted, accessed only by proctOR Medical Reviewers on a need-to-know basis, and never shared with other marketplace users. |
| Can I be a Mentor and a Mentee? | Yes — dual-role is supported, per specialty. |
| I'm a foreign-trained doctor in the EU. Can I join? | Yes, provided you hold a valid license to practice in your jurisdiction and, where applicable, a valid EU recognition (approbation) for sessions involving EU-based patients or institutions. |
