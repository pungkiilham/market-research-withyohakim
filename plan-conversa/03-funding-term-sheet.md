## 3. How SAFE Works (Simple Explanation)

```
You invest €10,000 today
     ↓
Company raises Series A later at €2,000,000 valuation
     ↓
Your €10,000 converts to equity:
  - At €500,000 cap → you get shares as if you invested at €500K
  - OR at 20% discount off €2M → you get shares as if at €1.6M
  - Whichever gives you MORE shares
     ↓
Result: ~2-3% ownership depending on round size
```
